# Grassroots Money: Club Karting, Local Short Tracks, Sponsors and Family Funding (US, 2015-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-09. All amounts are nominal US dollars for the stated year.

**Confidence levels**
- **high:** an official or primary document (track or club fee page, rulebook, purse sheet).
- **medium:** a trade outlet, or an itemized budget from a named person.
- **low:** a forum post, a classified ad, an anonymous or SEO blog, or a search-engine snippet that could not be opened.
- **EST:** my own estimate, with the arithmetic shown.

This file adds to `pyramid_economics_geography.md` §2.1/§5.3, `stock_car_and_dirt_pathways.md` §3/§14, and the payout tables in `RULES_AND_MONEY.md`. Those files already hold the short-track season budgets, the ARCA/NASCAR approval rules and the weekly purses. This file goes deeper on club karting, sponsorship and family funding, and gives a sanity check of the existing numbers.

## 1. Local kart club racing

### 1.1 Entry fees
- **KCKA, Liberty MO (2024).** Members pay $45 per class, non-members $85. Transponder rental is $10 for members and $20 for non-members, and a pit pass is $10. The season is 9 races with 8 scored, and a driver must finish 7 to be eligible for the title. Conf. high. https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/kcka-race-2-full-track-ccw-liberty-sprint-kansas-city-karting-association-228552
- **Kart Circuit Autobahn Karting Challenge, IL.**
  - 2024: $75 per class in advance, $85 at the track. Conf. low (search snippet of an official page). https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/kca-karting-challenge-round-1-kart-circuit-autobahn-905845
  - 2025: $80 / $90. A kart number for the year is $20 per class (one time), the weekend pit pass is $15, and a Friday practice pass is $60 per driver. The series has 6 rounds and 10 classes, including LO206 Cadet, Junior, Senior and Masters and Ignite. Conf. high. https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/kca-karting-challenge-round-1-kart-circuit-autobahn-061211

### 1.4 Season length and travel
- **Club race days per season.**
  - KCA: 6 rounds (2025).
  - KCKA: 9 (2024).
  - Pitt LO206 Cup: 10 (2020).
  - RAKC: 9 weekend + 12 weeknight (2024).
  - Racer plans: 9-15 race days, e.g. "9 weekends 18 race day" (2023, low). https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/kart-lo206-consumables-cost/10224
- **Travel.**
  - Club racers usually live close to the track. Examples: "live 5 miles from track" (2018); tolls and gas for 13 trips (2018). https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/a-cost-analysis-of-getting-into-karting/3026?page=2 (low)
  - "Race day costs about $200 including travel for local" (2021, low; same thread).
  - **EST travel cost for a 1-2 hour track:** 150 mi round trip ÷ 15 mpg × $3.50/gal ≈ $35 fuel, plus ~$30 food ≈ **$65 per race day**.

### 1.5 Tires
- **The rule is per club, not national.**
  - KCA 2026: one spec tire, the MG RL1 Orange, for all Briggs classes. https://kartcircuitautobahn.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-KCA-Karting-Challenge-Class-Structure-and-Rules-Rev-2.pdf (high)
  - Ignite classes run the Hoosier R80.
  - RAKC 2026: MG VK Orange for all Briggs classes. https://roadamerica.com/sites/main/files/file-attachments/2025_rakc_class_structure.pdf (high; the file served the 2026 rule book pages)
  - Some clubs require racing on the tires used to qualify, i.e. one set per day (a 2023 club rule-change proposal, low, snippet). https://www.caccautosport.org/forms/reports/cacc-rule-change-proposals-summary/entry/408/?pagenum=6
- **Prices and wear.**
  - Hoosier R80 set of 4: ~$295 (undated retailer, low). https://www.walmart.com/ip/393670177
  - "$250 tires when worn totally out" (2024, low). https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/how-much-cheaper-is-briggs-206-compared-to-other-series/12812
  - Wear reported by racers: 2-4 sets per club season; "3-4 races between replacement"; "4 sets ... 18 race day"; "new tires every 2-3 race weekends". Take-off (used) sets cost $20-50. https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/kart-lo206-consumables-cost/10224 (low, 2023)
  - **EST:** $280 per set ÷ 3 race days ≈ **$95 per race day**.

### 1.6 LO206 engine life and cost
- **Price.** A sealed crate engine retails at $600-860 (retailer listings, 2024-26, medium). https://pointkarting.com/products/briggs-stratton-lo206-engine-kart ; https://90racing.com/products/briggs-lo206-engine-ebl0010
  - "LO206 ~ 1000 race prepped new" (2024, low).
- **No rebuild interval.** You "cannot rebuild the engine in this class": you replace a sealed short block (~$300) and service the head when valve seats wear (2023, low). https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/kart-lo206-consumables-cost/10224 ; https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/top-end-clarification/6635
  - A family replaced the short block and freshened the head after about 3 seasons on the engine (2023, low; same thread).
  - A sealed short block costs "$600 rebuild vs $4-500" (low). https://4cycle.com/karting/threads/briggs-lo206-survey.116596/page-3
  - Head flow-bench, leak-down and service: $200 (2021, low). https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/a-cost-analysis-of-getting-into-karting/3026?page=2
- **Running cost per hour.**
  - LO206 ~$45-60 per hour all-in (engine maintenance ~$15/h, fuel and oil ~$10/h, tires ~$25/h, chain ~$3/h).
  - KA100 two-stroke ~$250-300 per hour.
  - 2024, low. https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/how-much-cheaper-is-briggs-206-compared-to-other-series/12812
- **Fuel.** About 1 gallon of pump gas per race day (2023, low; consumables thread).

- **What the purses mean for a racer.** In the entry classes the start money ($50-100) is about the same as the driver's own pit pass ($25-50) plus one crew pass. A back-marker roughly breaks even on gate fees and loses money on tires and fuel. EST, from the table.

## 3. How local racers are sponsored

### 3.1 Deal sizes
- **Wisconsin team guide (2026).**
  - Small decal or support partner: $300-1,500 for one race, or $1,500-4,000 a season. This buys a small logo on a quarter panel, rear deck or lower fender, one thank-you post, limited pit passes, and announcements.
  - Mid-tier: $4,000-12,000. Better placement, recurring social posts, some hospitality.
  - Primary: $12,000-35,000+ a season. Hood, door or top; appearances; hospitality.
  - Conf. low (a team's own marketing blog, Midwest short-track and regional teams). https://willmanracing.com/2026/03/27/wisconsin-race-sponsorship-cost/
- **Joe Verdegan, motorsports marketing column (Dec 2023).** Teams ask "$1,000, $2,000 or $5,000 for a hood", depending on the market. A touring late model commands more than a local street stock or 4-cylinder. He warns against "30 different $200 and $300 sponsors". Conf. medium. https://joeverdegan.com/what-is-the-value-of-your-racing-program/
- **SpeedwayMedia guide for local businesses (Sept 2026).** A logo on a local short-track car costs "a few hundred to a few thousand dollars per season". Hood and quarter panels are the most expensive spots. Conf. low. https://speedwaymedia.com/2026/09/22/how-to-sponsor-a-race-team-a-practical-guide-for-local-businesses/
- **Team rate card (~2021).** Partial primary (hood or both quarters) $7.5k and up; associate (behind the tire or on the posts) $1.5k and up. Conf. low. https://wolfpackracingusa.com/car-sponsorship-opportunities/
- **Older data points.**
  - Trailer lettering: $1,000 a year (forum, ~2009, low). https://www.racingjunk.com/forums/showthread.php?t=17223
  - The largest local sponsorship at Langley (VA) was up to ~$30k (1996, medium; see `team_economics.md`).
- **Karting.**
  - No US kart sponsor price list was found.
  - A 13-year-old's family spending ~$2,000 a year had 3 named sponsors: a T-shirt printer, a vinyl/sign shop and an indoor karting center. They were "family and friends, mostly" (2018, medium). https://vnews.com/?p=27747
  - Kartpulse sponsorship threads give no dollar figures. The advice is to start small, often with in-kind or work-for-trade deals (2021, low). https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/a-real-time-exercise-in-securing-sponsorship-for-kart-racers/6857 ; https://forums.kartpulse.com/t/how-would-you-use-kart-racing-to-market-sponsors/1822

### 3.3 Obligations, payment terms and how deals end
- **Obligations.**
  - Logo placement scaled to price, show-car appearances at the business, social posts and race reports, driver appearances, hauler, suit and crew-shirt branding, and pit or hospitality passes. Conf. low. SpeedwayMedia and Willman (links above).
  - Win updates, photos of decals in place, and monthly emails (DEI 2017, low; link above).
  - Spend about 20% of sponsor money on activation, "to keep the sponsor happy" (4cycle, 2014, low). https://4cycle.com/karting/threads/how-to-get-sponsors.31383/
- **Payment timing: no grassroots source found.**
  - A template contract lists "upfront or installments" as the choice to write down. Conf. low. https://www.mcsprogram.org/HomePages/u51F6H/246798/Sample Racing Sponsorship Contract.pdf
  - A pro dispute (Conway / Front Row, 2010) turned on per-race payments versus weekly installments. Conf. low (snippet). https://www.jayski.com/2010/10/24/conway-says-front-row-owes-him-money/
- **How deals end.** Guides say to test a single season before signing multi-year, and to include a refund or backup-car clause if the car misses races. Conf. low (SpeedwayMedia). In practice deals are annual and simply lapse. EST, see Gaps.

### 3.4 Number of sponsors and share of budget

- The kart family had 3 sponsors (vnews 2018).

## 5. Gates: ages, licences, approvals, rookie rules
(The national ARCA/NASCAR/USF and SCCA gates are covered in `stock_car_and_dirt_pathways.md` §3 and `open_wheel_sports_car_touring_club_pathways.md` §2.2. Only the local and club gates are listed here.)

- **Kart class ages (the age on Dec 31 counts).**
  - RAKC 2026:
    - Kid Kart 5-8
    - Briggs Cadet 7-10
    - Sportsman 8-12
    - Junior 12-15
    - Senior 15+
    - Masters 35+
    - Rule: "Cadet Champion must move to Sportsman class the next season". This is a forced move-up.
    - Drivers may enter only one of the aged classes Kid Kart, Cadet or Sportsman.
    - Conf. high. https://roadamerica.com/sites/main/files/file-attachments/2025_rakc_class_structure.pdf
  - KCA 2026:
    - Cadet 7-12
    - Junior 12-15
    - Senior 15+
    - Masters 30+ (or 15+ if over 200 lb)
    - Under-age drivers can ask in writing for a waiver, reviewed against their racing résumé.
    - Conf. high. https://kartcircuitautobahn.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-KCA-Karting-Challenge-Class-Structure-and-Rules-Rev-2.pdf
  - Pitt 2020: Kid Kart 5-8, Cadet 7-12, Junior 12-14, Senior 15+. Conf. high (Pitt link).
- **Club licence or membership.**
  - Club membership is needed for points (RAKC, Badger).
  - New members must attend a rookie school (Calgary, 2025).
  - Minors need a birth certificate and a parental release (Badger 2019, IKF).
  - WKA-sanctioned club events required WKA membership, or a one-event temporary membership at the track: $10 with the FKCS membership (2015, low). https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/fkcs-race-3-ocala-2015-series-grand-prix-kart-florida-karting-championship-057021
  - Current WKA, IKF and AKRA dues were not found. WKA has sold driver-only 12-month memberships via MSR since 2022 (low, snippet). https://www.ekartingnews.com/?p=112380
- **Local stock car licence.**
  - LVMS 2026: every Limited LM, Modified, Street, Factory and Mini Stock driver must hold a valid NASCAR licence; Legends and Bandoleros need an INEX licence. Conf. high (LVMS PDF).
  - NASCAR weekly minimum age was cut from 16 to 14 in 2010. Conf. low (Wikipedia, citation needed). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Local_Racing_Series
- **Rookie rules at local tracks.**
  - Skagit Speedway: rookies run a ribbon and start at the rear for 3 races, or until the series director signs them off. Conf. low (snippet of a rulebook). https://www.skagitspeedway.com/downloads/get.aspx?i=805068
  - NCQMA quarter midgets: a written move-up form for the rookie director at least a week before the first race in a competitive class. Conf. low (snippet of a rulebook). https://www.ncqma.com/?p=20598
  - Bandolero beginner moves up "after 2-4" or "8-14" races, depending on consistency. Conf. low (snippet). https://www.echoparkspeedway.com/media/news/drivers-get-their-start-thunder-ring-beginner-bandolero-class.html
  - NASCAR national series dropped rookie stripes for 2025. Conf. low (snippet). https://www.jalopnik.com/1976756/why-nascar-got-rid-of-rookie-stripes/
- **SCCA.** Novice permit from age 14. A driver without a road licence needs prior kart or quarter-midget experience. Then 1-2 schools and 2 races lead to a Regional licence. Conf. low (snippets of official SCCA pages; sources differ on 1 or 2 schools). https://www.scca.com/pages/enduro-drivers ; https://www.scca.com/pages/step-two
